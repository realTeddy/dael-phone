# Sound credits

## Animal sounds

All animal recordings (`app/src/main/res/raw/s_dog.ogg`, `s_cat`, `s_cow`, `s_horse`, `s_pig`, `s_sheep`,
`s_chicken`, `s_rooster`, `s_goose`, `s_donkey`, `s_goat`) are excerpts from the
[Farm Animal Sounds pack](https://orangefreesounds.com/farm-animal-sounds-sound-pack/) by
[Orange Free Sounds](https://orangefreesounds.com/), used under its free-for-commercial-use terms with
this backlink. Each clip was trimmed to about 2.5 seconds and loudness-normalized by `tools/process_sounds.py`.
Only a small subset of the pack is included, not the pack itself.
